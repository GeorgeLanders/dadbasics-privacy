Dad Basics - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version

Dad Basics collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and zero Android permissions. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing.

The app has no server. There is no account to create and no sign-in. We do not receive your data because there is nowhere for us to receive it from.

Android permissions

The app requests zero Android permissions. This was verified against the released app bundle, not assumed.

You can confirm it yourself: install Dad Basics, open the system permission screen for it, and you will find nothing listed.

What is stored on your device

These are kept in the app's private storage, readable only by Dad Basics:

DataWhy it existsWhere it lives
Favourite and completed lessonsTo show you your progressOn device
Checklists you start and tick offTo remember what is doneOn device
Child profiles (name and age)To suggest age-appropriate lessonsOn device
Your own AI provider key, if you add oneTo authenticate your requestsOn device

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

The optional AI feature

Dad Basics has an optional Ask screen. It is off until you turn it on, and it requires a key that you supply yourself.

When you use it:

  • The question you type, together with your child profile (name and age, if you

have set one), is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you chose. It does not pass through us, because we run no server.

  • Your provider key is stored only on your device and is sent only to that

provider.

  • The provider's own privacy policy and terms govern what happens to your

question once it reaches them. We have no control over that and do not see it.

Every other feature - all 20 lessons, all checklists, progress tracking - works with no key, no account and no network connection at all.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for parents and carers, not for children to use directly. Child profiles hold a first name and an age only. We do not collect information from children, and because we collect nothing from anyone, there is nothing for us to hold.

Medical disclaimer

Dad Basics contains general parenting and first-aid guidance. It is not medical advice and it does not replace a qualified professional. Lessons covering health topics are marked CHECK WITH A PROFESSIONAL. Always consult a doctor or pharmacist for advice about a specific child.

Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. To remove everything the app holds, uninstall it, or clear its storage in your device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and the date at the top will change. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
